# Present

### 11. `[S]` Present, rehearse, keep time

*Summary:* What the projector does, in three columns.

*Text:* 125%

*Layout:* three

#### In the room

**P** presents, on the projector when one is plugged in; **D** moves it to the other display.

**N** shows the notes for this click, **O** every slide at once.

<!-- col -->

#### Rehearsing

**H** shows the hidden slides too. The next slide is one.

A note can start with the time the slide should be reached at.

<!-- col -->

#### On its own

`?auto=20` moves on every 20 seconds, at a PechaKucha's pace; **A** pauses.

[Watch this deck do it](https://kordikp.github.io/slaidy/?auto=5#present)

### 12. `[S]` For the speaker only

*Summary:* A hidden slide, shown only when rehearsing with H or editing.

*Text:* 125%

*Skip:* yes

You see this slide because you pressed **H**, or because you are editing. In the room it is
skipped, and the slide list shows it struck through.

Keep the backup here: the answer to the question you expect, the slide you cut for time, the
teacher's key.
