Add your theme

A theme is a folder with a stylesheet, a few lines of JSON and its fonts. Bring your university's, your company's or your own look:

  1. Fork kordikp/slaidy and make themes/<name>/ with theme.css, theme.json and the fonts with their licence.
  2. python3 scripts/themes.py builds it with the sample deck; python3 scripts/theme_preview.py <name> makes the picture for this page.
  3. Run tests/all.sh and open a pull request. Once it is merged, it is here and in every editor's theme list.
