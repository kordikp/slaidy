# Code of conduct

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to make the thing better.

Concretely: critique the code, not the person; say what you would do instead;
accept that a maintainer may say no to a change that is fine in itself but wrong for
this project. Harassment, personal attacks and deliberate bad faith are not welcome
here and will get you removed.

If something needs attention, mail kordik@gmail.com. It will stay between us unless
you say otherwise.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), 2.1.
