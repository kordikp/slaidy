### 6. `[S]` A chart is a drawing too

*Summary:* Figures are SVG, charts included, which is why they can be edited and why the deck is small.

*Layout:* two

*Flags:* center

Figures are SVG: text a model can read, git can diff and you can edit. A chart is no exception.
This one is a few dozen lines, and its bars grow by CSS alone.

Ask for "a log scale" or "add the PDF" and it comes back as a change to those lines, not as a new picture.

> Small follows from legible: 237 slides in 1 MB.

### 7. `[S]` Edit on the slide

*Summary:* You edit on the slide itself, at the size the room will see.

*Layout:* two

*Flags:* center

![[fig-figure-editing]]

<!-- col -->

Click a title to change it. Hover a paragraph for a grip to drag it, a `＋` to insert below it.

Click a figure and its editor opens on the drawing: select a shape, move it, recolour it, join
two boxes with an arrow that re-routes itself when either moves.

> Every gesture is one `Ctrl-Z`.
